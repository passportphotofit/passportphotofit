## PassportPhotoFit

Free passport, visa and ID photos to the official size, made **in your browser**. Your photo is never uploaded.

- Website: [passportphotofit.com](https://passportphotofit.com)
- 41 documents and 49 photo sizes (US, UK, Ireland, Schengen, Canada, Australia, India and more), each linked to its official government source
- Open data: [passport-photo-specs](https://github.com/passportphotofit/passport-photo-specs), photo sizes and rules as JSON and CSV (CC BY 4.0)
